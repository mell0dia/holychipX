# GM Card — Approved Phrases

The daily GM card rotates through these. Two go out a day: one at 08:57, just
ahead of the 09:00 story or vault, and one at 14:00. Phrases are DELETED from
this file as they are used, so nothing repeats and the file always shows what is
left. Survivors renumber themselves automatically.

Edit freely: delete a line to drop it, rewrite to taste, or add your own.
One phrase per numbered line. The `(HC0xx)` tag is just the source for reference
— it is stripped before rendering and never appears on the card.

Curated by hand 2026-09-09 and shuffled, so the order carries no theme and
consecutive days do not cluster on one story. Every line is copied VERBATIM from
that story's origin blog and none had been used on a card before.

`pick_thought()` falls back silently to a whole random blog paragraph — that is
why older cards ran to 265 characters. It does not break, it just stops being
curated. Refill before it runs dry.
