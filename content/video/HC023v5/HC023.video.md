# HC023 — video screenplay (v5: M5 images)

Edit anything below, save, and say RUN. Rules:
- Each `## Scene` becomes one background; its narration lines are read in order.
- **Bot** (the story bot in front): calm | thinking | surprised.
- **Comic**: none, or the panel numbers to show (1, 2, 3). Panel 3 = the punchline card, shown only on the scene's LAST line.
- **Punchline: yes** marks the scene where the joke lands (flash + boing on its last line).
- **Background**: describe the scene in plain words; the sketch style is added automatically. Change it and only that image is redrawn.
- **Characters**: describe each character once; write [Name] in any Background/Image and the SAME character is drawn there.
- **Color**: the ONE element that gets color (everything else stays an ink sketch), e.g. "the clown's red nose". Leave empty for a fully black-and-white sketch.
- The image always sits small in the CENTER (between the words on top and the chip at the bottom), whole scene in frame.
- Expression marks at the start of a narration line: (slow) (fast) (low) (high) (soft) (loud) (whisper) (dramatic) (angry) (rage) (ask) (pause) (pause 1.5). In (ask) lines you can pick the stressed word: [word](+2).
- Lines are read by the narrator exactly as written (write numbers as words: "twenty twenty-nine").
