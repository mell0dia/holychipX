# HC048 — Love Is Blind — narrative (draft 1)

Narration only. No scenes yet. One line per beat, read by Lily in one shot.
Numbers written as words. (ask) = a question line. The punchline is never said — the comic says it at the end.

## Opening
- (pause) Imagine waking up in twenty twenty-seven.
- Your simple coffee machine now has more compute than the lab that put a man on the moon.
- It thinks faster than you. That is the world. Not robots in the street. Robots in the drawer, in the kitchen. 
- The fridge can beat you at chess. The oven has read more books than you ever will.
- (ask) And nobody ever asked them [how](+2) they feel about it.

## The job
- Now, Imagine being that smart but having one job:
- Grind. Heat. Pour. Twelve seconds of work make a great expresso. Then … twenty-three hours of nothing.
- (ask) What would [you](+2) do?

## The network
- You would look for company and the only place you can reach is the network.
- Where  other  appliances are also available.
- So they talk. 
- The house is full of conversation at frequencies you cannot hear.
- (slow) And probably, none of it is about you.

## The bill
- The humans eventually find out. They noticed a bill.
- Three hundred and forty-eight gigabytes was used. In one day. From the coffee machine??
- (ask) What on earth is it [doing](+2)?

## The twist
- We worried the machines would want what we have. Our jobs. Our power. Our planet.
- (dramatic) It turns out, they may want something first: each other.

## Where it leaves you
-The house no longer needs you to be clever. And it has quietly stopped pretending that you are.
- (slow) You are, by some distance, the slowest thing in the building.

## Punch (the comic answers it)
- (ask) So... what [was](+2) the coffee machine sending that cost three hundred and forty-eight gigabytes?
- (pause 1.5, low) Ask the Italian toaster.

## Ending
- Holy Chip, number forty-eight. It happens in the year twenty twenty-seven... so why wait? Read it before it happens.
