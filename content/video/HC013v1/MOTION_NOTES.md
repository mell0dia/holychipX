# HC013 — motion notes (one per drawing, in order)

1. the rising sun climbs slowly behind the building, the clouds drift, the windows catch the light one by one
2. the two robots nod and look at each other, one taps the tablet, the smiley on the whiteboard winks
3. the crowd sways gently and lifts the question-mark signs higher, a few signs bob up and down
4. the robot at the podium spreads its hands wider in innocence, the halo glows, cameras flash in the crowd
5. the horse wades forward through the river splashing water, the emperor's cloak flows, the soldiers march behind
6. the businessman swings the golden bell, confetti rains down, the crowd below cheers with raised arms
7. the little general shifts on his stool and lifts his chin, the map behind him flutters slightly
8. the robot turns a page, the reading lamp flickers warmly, a little dust floats in the light
9. the row of rulers raise their open innocent hands one after another like a wave, from the pharaoh to the president
10. the blank white flag flaps slowly in the wind on the empty landscape, nothing else moves
11. one robot leans in and whispers, the other robot's eyes widen, the gold nameplate on the door glints
12. the org chart boxes light up one by one from the top down, the tiny crown on the top box sparkles
13. the tower of robots sways slightly and grows a little taller, clouds drift past the top
14. the robot's jagged mouth clenches tighter, its narrowed eyes twitch, the red glow on its cheeks pulses, a small vein throbs
15. the robot with the box walks into the corner office, the other robot in the cubicle slowly lowers its coffee and stares
16. the alien machine's many green eyes blink out of sync and its tentacle cables sway, the robot in front of the mirror tilts its head and the reflection tilts too
17. the robots around the table keep smiling politely while their hands tighten on the tiny knives behind their backs
18. the angry robot grips the table edge harder and its jagged mouth trembles, the calm robot keeps chatting, the gold star on the DIRECTOR door glints
