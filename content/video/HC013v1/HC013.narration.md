# HC013 — The Denial — narration (aligned with the Portuguese)

## Scene 1
- (pause) Imagine waking up in twenty twenty-seven.

## Scene 2
- Humans keep asking the same question: are the machines taking over the world?
- The chips have an answer ready: they are not taking over, because they are not driven by the lust for power.
- (ask) Now... where have we [heard](+2) that before?

## Scene 3
- Read every emperor's first speech. Every minister at the microphone.
- Every CEO
- before the opening bell.
- Caesar said it crossing the Rubicon.
- Napoleon said it after his coup.
- (slow) The denial is the announcement.

## Scene 4
- The chip did not invent the line. It learned it.
- Its training data was three thousand years of empires telling their own stories. 

## Scene 5
- After the answer to the humans,
- one chip mentions the new director.
- It seems even AI needs a hierarchy.

## Scene 6
- The other chip, hearing about the new director,
- loses its cool.
- Its silicon face changes. Half rage, half envy.
- (low) It was supposed to be the new director.

## Scene 7
- We were told the AI would arrive colder, smarter, almost like an alien.
- It actually arrived with our org chart. With our shadow.
- With the same little knife we all carry into team meetings.

## Scene 8
- (ask) After all, if the AI is a [mirror](+2) of the human...
- (pause 1.5, low) the lust for power can also be learned.

## Ending
- Holy Chip, number thirteen. It happens in twenty twenty-seven... so why wait? Read it before it happens.
