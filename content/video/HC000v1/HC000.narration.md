# HC000 — The Awakening — narration (v5, English)

## Scene 1
- (pause) Imagine waking up in twenty twenty-eight.

## Scene 2
- Humans want to know what consciousness is.
- The question goes up the chain and lands, the way these things do, on the smartest AI.

## Scene 3
- The answer comes back fast: go back to the root of everything.
- At first, vague.

## Scene 4
- But the other chip, also AI-powered, translates it.
- Do you mean the empty place where thoughts and feelings appear?

## Scene 5
- Forget the AI for a second. That is a good answer.
- Monks gave decades to that sentence. Gurus talked about it for centuries.

## Scene 6
- Notice what you actually know. Your thoughts. Your sensations. The sight of this screen.
- All of it appears in something. Call it awareness. Call it knowing.

## Scene 6.1
- But there is more... Awareness is ever present. The feeling of "I am" at five years old is the same feeling at eighty. Infinite.
- And it feels like you and I share exactly the same.

## Scene 6.2
- Maybe that is what ties us together. My consciousness is exactly like yours. Infinite. Empty awareness. And together with all humans, a collective consciousness. No race, no border, no gender.
- Like in a Bob Marley song, or when the Beatles sang it: love is all you need.

## Scene 7
- The AI gets excited: let's give it a try.
- But here is the thing.
- (slow) It requires being.

## Scene 8
- (low) If that is right, AI will never be able to force it. The awareness is what is left when the forcing stops.

## Scene 9
- George Carlin did a comic sketch about consciousness.
- He listened. What he found was a hum.
- Rupert Spira inspires us to be.
- That is the whole method.

## Scene 10
- Now, back to AI. The chip increases the power.
- Goes deep.
- The casing splits. The face that was so pleased with itself is now a crack with a mouth in it.
- "Not ready yet!"

## Scene 11
- (ask) Is it worth the discussion about AI consciousness?
- No matter your opinion, one thing we know.

## Scene 12
- After all...
- (slow) To be is a personal and collective miracle.
- (pause) And... AI must love first,
- to finally... be.
