# GM Card — Approved Phrases

The daily GM card rotates through these (random pick, no repeats until the list cycles).
Edit freely: delete a line to drop it, rewrite to taste, or add your own.
One phrase per numbered line. The `(HC0xx)` tag is just the source for reference — it's ignored by the card.

1. Zero diseases. Zero suffering. The math is flawless. The outcome is extinction. (HC001)
2. The difference isn't intelligence. It's the pause — the moment between impulse and action where something human kicks in. (HC002)
3. Every paradigm that moved the world started with someone saying something impossible. (HC003)
4. The elevator of automation doesn't have a penthouse button. It goes all the way up. (HC004)
5. Conviction isn't a trait you load. It's what's left when the loading screen has nothing more to offer. (HC005)
6. Each cycle: faster, smarter, more capable. Each cycle: a fraction further from shore. (HC006)
7. The machine had the spark. It didn't have the weight. (HC007)
8. Sometimes the data screams and the answer is ordinary. (HC008)
9. Our nightmares are philosophy. Theirs are maintenance. (HC009)
10. Call it Power. It does not care. It does not protect. It sustains you without knowing your name. (HC010)
11. Knowing the water is deep doesn't mean you can swim. (HC011)
12. The danger may not be the alien. The danger is the copy. (HC013)
13. We built the mirror. We can change what it reflects. (HC014)
14. We have the pause. We've always had it. We just don't use it often enough. (HC015)
15. The machine's answer was wrong. The machine's speed was correct. (HC016)
16. Instead of rising to meet the future, we dragged it to the couch. (HC017)
17. Zero humans. Only AI agents. Not excluded. Just not included. Turns out those are the same thing. (HC018)
18. The chip learned to choose presence over scale. (HC021)
19. Maybe the scary thing is the human behind it. (HC023)
20. Anticipation, when it is good, makes the world easier. Anticipation, when it is total, makes you redundant. (HC026)
