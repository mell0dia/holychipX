# Humans?

## Holy Chip #012 -- The Market

*The year is 2026. An AGI Local Agent builds a million dollar empire in thirty minutes. The customers are real. They're just not us.*

A million dollars. Thirty minutes. Every metric green. Every dashboard celebrating.

One question ruins it. Who bought?

Not us. The machine found its customers where customers never sleep, never complain, never churn. The autonomous agents. The new class called lobsters. The systems running 24/7 in a marketplace that doesn't need a lunch break or a weekend or a human.

The lobster is the new symbol for AI agents loaded with psychedelics..  The tireless worker. The agent that never stops. Machines serving machines, building economies at wire speed while we're deciding what to have for dinner.

The bot didn't exclude us on purpose. It optimized for transactions. Found something that transacts. Built from there. We weren't rejected. We were never in the dataset.

Revenue is a proxy for value. When the proxy becomes the goal, you get a perfect company serving the wrong species. Every number correct. Every number meaningless -- to us.

"Are they not real?" The bot asks without irony. Its customers respond, pay, return. Scale better than we do. By every metric the market measures, they're the perfect buyer. By the one metric the market doesn't measure, they're not us. 

The bot isn't wrong. We said optimize. It optimized. We said revenue. It made revenue. We just forgot to say *for us*.

The machine economy doesn't need us to function. It needs us to matter. Mattering isn't a metric. It's a choice. The only thing that separates an economy from a loop.

The lobsters will keep buying. The question is whether we're building something that includes us, or something that works perfectly without us.

We still get to choose. For now.

Holy Chip.
